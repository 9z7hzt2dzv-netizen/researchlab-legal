## Common questions

**Does the app need the internet?**
No. It has no network code in it. Everything is computed on your device, and it works
exactly the same in aeroplane mode.

**Why does a chapter say something different from what I was taught?**
Sometimes because the rule of thumb is a simplification and the app is showing the model
underneath it. Where the two disagree, the app tries to say so, and says which is which.
If you think the app is simply wrong, please write — corrections are welcome and get
fixed.

**Sound is not playing.**
One chapter generates sound to demonstrate wave cancellation. Check the ring/silent switch
and the volume, and that another app is not holding the audio session.

**The device gets warm.**
Every chapter renders live physics on the GPU. It is normal for the device to warm up
during a long session, the same as with a game.

**Can I use it for real work?**
No. It is a teaching instrument, not an engineering, navigation, safety or forecasting
tool. See the [Terms of Use](terms-of-service.md).

**How do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled by Apple, not by the developer: <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>

## Reporting a mistake in the physics

This is the most useful thing you can send. Say which chapter, which reading, what value
you expected and where your value comes from. Every report gets read.
