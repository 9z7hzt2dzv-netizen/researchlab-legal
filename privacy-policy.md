# Privacy Policy

**ResearchLab**
Last updated: 12 August 2026

## The short version

**ResearchLab collects nothing.**

The app has no network code in it. It cannot send anything anywhere, because there is
nothing in it that opens a connection. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics,
no advertising, no third-party SDKs and no tracking of any kind.

Everything you see is computed on your device, from physics written into the app itself.

## What the app stores on your device

A small number of preferences, kept in the app's own sandbox using the standard iOS
preferences store. Specifically:

- which introductory cards you have dismissed, so a card you have read is not shown again
- the settings you last left a simulation on, such as a temperature or a viewing distance
- which chapters you have finished, and which you have marked as liked

None of it identifies you. None of it leaves the device. All of it is deleted when you
delete the app.

## What the app does not do

- It does not use the network, at all.
- It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photos, health
  data, or any other sensor or personal database.
- It does not use the device advertising identifier, and it does not ask for tracking
  permission, because it does not track.
- It does not contain analytics, crash-reporting or attribution frameworks.
- It does not show advertising.
- It does not contain in-app purchases or subscriptions.

## Sound

One chapter plays sound it generates on the device, to demonstrate how two waves cancel.
It only plays; it never records, and the app has no microphone permission to ask for.

## Purchases

ResearchLab is a paid app bought from the App Store. The purchase is handled entirely by
Apple. We never see your payment details, your Apple Account, or your name. Apple may
provide the developer with aggregate, anonymous sales and usage reports, subject to your
own App Store privacy settings, which you control in Settings on your device.

## Children

The app is suitable for readers of about fourteen and up. It collects no data from anyone,
of any age, which means it collects none from children either.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version is published at this address and the date
at the top changes with it. Because the app collects nothing, no change here can
retroactively affect data that was never gathered.
