# AI and How This App Was Made

**First Principles: Physics**
Last updated: 12 August 2026

## There is no AI in the app

First Principles: Physics contains no artificial intelligence, no machine learning models and no
generative features. It never sends anything to a server, because it has no network code
in it at all.

Every picture the app draws is computed on your device from physics: published equations,
solved as you move a control. When a chapter says a hurricane's ceiling is 42.6 m/s, that
number came out of the model a moment earlier. Nothing in the app produces text or images
on the fly, and nothing it shows was generated by a language model while you were reading.

## How it was written

The app was built by a human developer using AI coding assistants, in the same way most
software is written today — for drafting code, drafting explanations and checking work.
Every model, number, equation and sentence was reviewed by a person before it shipped, and
the app verifies its own physics against independent derivations at runtime.

This is said plainly because it should be, not because it changes what the app does.

## What that means for you

- The physics comes from published scientific work, not from a model's recollection of it.
- The explanations were written to be read by a person and were checked by one.
- Mistakes are still possible. See the accuracy section of the
  [Terms of Use](terms-of-service.md), and please report anything that looks wrong.
