# First Principles: Physics

**More than twenty pieces of physics, simulated on your device and explained from scratch.**

Not videos. Not diagrams. Every picture here is live physics, solved on your
iPhone or iPad while you move a control, from the same published equations the field uses.
Move a slider and the model is re-solved and redrawn — because it was never a picture in
the first place.

No account. No internet connection. No advertising. No in-app purchases. It works in
aeroplane mode, because it has no network code in it at all.

Written for a curious reader of about fourteen and up, with no background assumed.

## How it works

Each chapter is built the same way:

- **A model, not an animation.** The physics is solved live. A hurricane's ceiling comes
  out of a cubic; a tree's water column comes out of a hydraulic solve; the Sun's outflow
  temperature is found by lifting a parcel against a real tropical sounding.
- **Every number is computed.** If a reading says 42.6 m/s, the model produced it a moment
  before you read it. Nothing is typed in to look right.
- **The app checks itself.** Each chapter's model is verified against independent
  derivations — a second method, a published table, a closed form — before anything is
  drawn.
- **Controls are places, not sliders.** Each control notches at values that mean something,
  and the readout says what the state you are in actually is, including when the answer is
  *nothing happens, and here is why*.
